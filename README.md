## 📑 Academic Contribution — Data Science

### Academic Performance, Course Load, and Student Persistence

**Conference:** 24th LACCEI International Multi-Conference for Engineering, Education and Technology — 2026

**Abstract:** Longitudinal study applying **logistic regression and statistical data modeling** to analyze academic trajectories at ITM. The study identifies key factors influencing student dropout and persistence, with implications for institutional intervention strategies.

**Methods used:**
- Logistic regression for binary outcome modeling (persist / dropout)
- Feature engineering from historical academic records
- Cohort analysis across multiple enrollment periods
- Statistical significance testing and model validation

**Keywords:** Educational Data Mining · Student Retention · Logistic Regression · Higher Education Analytics

## 🧪 Experimental Physics

### Determination of Neon Excitation Energy — Franck–Hertz Experiment

Experimental implementation of the Franck–Hertz experiment to measure the excitation energy of neon atoms. Electrons are accelerated through a neon-filled tube; current dips occur at integer multiples of the excitation energy.

**Techniques:**
- Current–voltage (I–V) curve acquisition and analysis
- Peak-to-peak interval measurement for energy determination
- Uncertainty propagation using standard error methods

---

### Determination of the Velocity of a Sound Source — Doppler Effect

Experimental measurement of source velocity using the acoustic Doppler effect. A moving sound source is recorded and its frequency shift is analyzed to compute velocity.

**Techniques:**
- Audio signal capture and preprocessing
- Frequency analysis via **Fourier Transform (FFT)**
- Doppler equation fitting to measured frequency shifts
- Uncertainty analysis and result validation
